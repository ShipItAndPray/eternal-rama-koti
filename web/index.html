Three traditions, one book

Rama Koti is the Telugu practice of writing శ్రీరామ a crore times, with completed books offered at the Sita Ramachandra Swamy temple in Bhadrachalam. Sri Rama Jayam is the Tamil practice of writing ஸ்ரீ ராம ஜெயம், page after page, for years. Ram Naam Lekhan is the North Indian practice of writing राम, with books deposited at the Ram Naam Bank in Varanasi since 1926. This is one shared book for all three, written on Ethereum, where nothing written can be lost.

The book has no owner, no admin, and no server. The contract cannot be paused, edited, or taken down by anyone. Every name you write is minted to your address as an NFT that lives entirely on Ethereum, image included.
